## COM2039 Parallel Computing
University of Surrey, second-year undergraduate module.
{: .course-meta}

How to write programs that use many processors at once, with GPU programming in CUDA as the main vehicle. The module covers the limits of parallel speed-up (Amdahl's and Gustafson's laws), the GPU execution and memory model, occupancy, and the standard parallel patterns — map, reduce, scan, histogram — and how to implement them efficiently.

Course materials are on SurreyLearn for enrolled students.

## Deep Neural Networks
Department of Computer Science, University of Oxford, [Software Engineering Programme](https://www.cs.ox.ac.uk/softeng/subjects/DNN.html).
{: .course-meta}

A short course for practising software engineers on the fundamentals of deep neural networks: how they learn, the main architectures (convolutional and recurrent networks) and where they apply, the practical issues in training them, implementing back-propagation from scratch, and where deep learning sits among other machine learning methods.
