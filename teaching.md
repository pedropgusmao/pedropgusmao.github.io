## COM2039 Parallel Computing (2024 - current )
University of Surrey, second-year undergraduate module.
{: .course-meta}

This module covers the core aspects of Parallel Computing including GPU execution and memory model, occupancy, and the standard parallel patterns such as reduce, scan, convolutions, histogram. It combines theoretical foundations with hands-on practice where we develop GPU-accelerated applications using CUDA, analyse their performance characteristics, and learn to think differently about computational problems.

Course materials are available on SurreyLearn for enrolled students.

## Deep Neural Networks (2020 - 2022)
Department of Computer Science, University of Oxford, [Software Engineering Programme](https://www.cs.ox.ac.uk/softeng/subjects/DNN.html).
{: .course-meta}

This was an intensive course on the fundamentals of deep neural networks: how they are trained, the main architectures (convolutional and recurrent networks) and where they apply, the practical issues in training them, implementing back-propagation from scratch, and where deep learning sits among other machine learning methods.
