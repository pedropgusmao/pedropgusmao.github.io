About

I am a Lecturer in Computer Science at the University of Surrey, where I am part of the Nature Inspired Computing and Engineering (NICE) group. My research is on federated learning, machine unlearning, and privacy-preserving machine learning more broadly.

Before joining Surrey, I was a Senior Research Associate at the University of Cambridge, where I was one of the core developers of Flower, the open-source federated learning framework. Before that, I was a postdoctoral researcher at the University of Oxford, working on localisation and navigation for emergency responders. I hold a PhD from Politecnico di Torino and a BSc in Electrical Engineering from the University of São Paulo.
